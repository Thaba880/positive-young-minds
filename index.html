About Positive Young Minds

Positive Young Minds is a Nonprofit Organisation based at 7 Rex Street, Roodepoort, 1724, Johannesburg, serving young people in schools, communities and orphanages across South Africa. Governed by a Board of Directors. Operates on donations and grants.

🎯 Mission

Building Our Tomorrow Today.

👁️ Vision

For every young individual to make a difference.

🛡️ Scam Safety

PYM does not charge for public services. Joining is voluntary. If someone asks you to pay to join PYM, report it to 066 563 2663.
