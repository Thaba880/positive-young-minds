Who we are

Growing stronger, together.

Positive Young Minds is a youth organisation based in Johannesburg, Gauteng, serving young people across South Africa. We believe lasting progress happens when young people are supported, equipped and empowered to shape their own futures.

🎯 Our Mission

To equip young people with the mindset, skills, support and opportunities they need to succeed in education, work and life.

👁️ Our Vision

A South Africa where every young person believes in themselves, has access to opportunity, and can build a positive future.

💎 Our Values

Positivity • Growth • Respect • Resilience • Community • Excellence
We see potential, not problems.

Who is it for? Learners in Grade 10–12 • TVET / University students • Unemployed youth • Young job seekers • Volunteers & mentors • Schools & community organisations
