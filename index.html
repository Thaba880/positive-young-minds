About

Positive Young Minds is a nonprofit that shows up where young people are.

Based at 7 Rex Street, Roodepoort, 1724, Johannesburg, serving young people in schools, communities and orphanages across South Africa. Governed by a Board of Directors. Operates on donations and grants.

🎯 Mission

Building Our Tomorrow Today.

👁️ Vision

For every young individual to make a difference.

🛡️ Scam Safety

PYM does not charge for public services. Joining is voluntary. If someone asks you to pay to join PYM, report it to 066 563 2663.

Our identity

Four pillars. One purpose.

1. Empower Young Minds

Confidence, voice and belief that you can lead.

2. Educate For Success

Practical skills, learning and preparation for life and work.

3. Collaborate For Impact

Schools, partners and communities working together.

4. Inspire Positive Change

Young people who lift others as they rise.

What PYM actually does

PYM runs Mental Health Awareness and Youth Empowerment Conferences, Leadership Empowerment Sessions, STEPi workshops, Masterclass training, sports tournaments and awards — all in schools and communities.
